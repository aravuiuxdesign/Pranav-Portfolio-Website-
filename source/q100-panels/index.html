— Overview

Q100 is a staff app for hotel housekeeping. It turns room checks into a guided flow: the worker sees what's assigned, compares each area against a reference photo with AI help, and submits a time-stamped record managers can trust.

— Problem and Solution

Room inspections usually live in memory, paper checklists and verbal handoffs. Standards drift between floors and shifts, and nobody can prove a room was checked properly.

Problem

Housekeepers don't know exactly what "passed" looks like, rely on memory for every room, and have no simple way to prove their work. Managers walk every floor to check, and issues surface only when a guest complains.

Solution

A guided inspection flow. Each area shows its reference photo and checklist, AI checks every photo against the standard, and every room leaves a time-stamped record the whole team can see.

— App Screens

Seventeen worker screens across the full shift: from first launch, through every inspection, to the end-of-day summary.

Onboarding

First launch explains what Q100 does for housekeeping staff in three short promises.

Log in & access

Sign in with email or Employee ID, or hotel SSO. Recovery stays self-serve so a forgotten password never blocks a shift.

Home

Shift progress, the room in progress and anything sent back, all readable in a corridor glance. Up next shows the rooms that matter first.

Guided inspection

The core moment. The reference and checklist stay in view while AI checks each photo and shows its confidence, so staff fix issues before moving on.

Insights & records

Reports turn numbers into one clear insight. History keeps every inspection, scored and searchable.

Profile & real-world states

Personal stats and settings, plus the states that matter on a real shift: working offline with a safe upload queue.

01About the project

Parallel brings a crew into one room to watch a film in sync, with voice and text chat alongside the story.

A personal app concept. The room is the centre: one film, one shared night.

09Sign-in and onboarding

No password to remember.

Enter a phone number, confirm the six-digit code and choose a crew name.

Before the app begins.

The bottom bar stays out of sign-in. It appears only after entering the app.

Pawssible — adoption app case study

Pawssible is an adoption app that makes discovering pets, posting rescues and finding them homes simple. Adopters browse available pets, send private enquiries and track what happens next; posters share photos and details, and listings are reviewed before going live. The case study covers the problem, adoption alternatives, design process, personas, user flow, screen structure, typography, colours, branding and the full set of 56 screens.

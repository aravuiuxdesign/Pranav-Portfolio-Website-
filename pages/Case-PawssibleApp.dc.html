About the app

A mobile app concept for Pawssible. Discover pets, send an enquiry and share a rescue, all in one friendly adoption experience.

The challenge

Adoption posts get buried in a busy WhatsApp group. Details are scattered, and it is hard to see which pets are still waiting for a home.

Problem & Solution

?
Problem

New messages push pets out of sight. Adopters have to piece together the story and check whether a listing is still current.

✦
Solution

One organised adoption experience brings current pet profiles, private enquiries and clear adoption updates together.

Pawssible concept

The concept responds to the gaps in a scattered adoption journey: visibility, current information and a clear next step.

Visibility

Available pets stay findable instead of disappearing beneath new posts.

Clarity

Structured profiles bring photos, needs and adoption status into one place.

Tracking

Designed to keep the next step visible.

  • Enquiry reference and tracker
  • Listing review status
  • Adoption confirmation
  • Happy Endings archive

Design process

From the WhatsApp problem to a complete screen system, with pet visibility as the guiding aim.

  1. 01Idea

    Bring buried adoption posts into one maintained place.

  2. 02Understanding

    Identify visibility, trust and handover needs.

  3. 03Structure

    Map six journeys and their decision points.

  4. 04UI design

    Build the screen system and shared patterns.

  5. 05Refinement

    Review alignment, spacing and interface states.

Screen structure

The ten core screens from the final UI, together in the app's real colours.

Mobile app

The interface is designed to be clear and welcoming, making adoption simple, organised and personal.

01Onboarding

Get started with Pawssible.

Discover pets, post a pet, celebrate adoption. Three simple promises before the first profile.

Three promises
02Sign in / guest

A little hello. A clear place to start.

Sign in to enquire and post, or continue as a guest. Phone numbers and email stay private to the foundation team.

Private by default
03Explore

Everything your pet needs.

A nearest-first feed with search, species chips and clear availability, designed to keep the right pets visible.

Nearest first
04Filters

Find the right pet.

Age, size, temperament and distance, phrased in everyday language.

Species / Age / Gender / Location
05Pet profile

Know your new friend.

Photos, story, care information and one clear enquiry action. Public profiles do not display personal phone numbers.

One clear action
06Enquiry

Start a conversation.

A short enquiry form gathers the details needed for the next step.

Your details stay private
07Enquiries

Keep track.

References and clear statuses show where each enquiry stands, so the next chapter does not get lost in the chat.

Enquiry sent → In review → Next steps
08Post a pet

Share a pet.

Photos, basics, details and review turn a rescue story into a structured listing.

Photos / Basics / Details
09Review

Review before posting.

The full listing can be checked before it is submitted. A volunteer reviews it before it goes live.

Ready for one final check
10Volunteer review

Volunteer review.

A proposed review queue for approving listings or asking for specific changes. Access is granted by the foundation.

Approve or request changes
11Adopted

Found a home.

Celebrate the adoption, then move the pet to Happy Endings so the feed only shows pets still waiting.

Available → Adopted
